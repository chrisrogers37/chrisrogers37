I build small, sharp tools and give them questionable names. A Spotify playlist fixer, an Instagram-Story-via-Telegram bot, a Mac that finally sleeps, a thing that mines Truth Social for trading alpha (the branding is a work in progress). Mostly Python and TypeScript, a little Swift when a Mac annoys me enough.

## 🤖 The part nobody else has

Most of these repos don't get shipped by hand. I run **claudlobby** — an autonomous fleet of Claude Code agents (a manager, engineers, a designer, reviewers) running on cheap hardware. They pick up tasks, open branches, write the code, review each other's pull requests, and ship end-to-end while I'm doing something else.

This README? Drafted by one of them. 🤝

## 📦 Projects

### [shuffify](https://github.com/chrisrogers37/shuffify)
![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white) ![Flask](https://img.shields.io/badge/Flask-000000?style=flat-square&logo=flask&logoColor=white) ![Spotify](https://img.shields.io/badge/Spotify-1DB954?style=flat-square&logo=spotify&logoColor=white) ![Tailwind](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tailwind-06B6D4?style=flat-square&logo=tailwindcss&logoColor=white) ![Docker](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docker-2496ED?style=flat-square&logo=docker&logoColor=white)

Spotify playlists, minus the decision paralysis. Reshuffle, dedupe, and reorder your library through a clean web UI — Flask on the backend, Tailwind up front, Dockerized so it just runs.

### [really-personal-finance](https://github.com/chrisrogers37/really-personal-finance)
![Next.js](https://img.shields.io/badge/Next.js-black?style=flat-square&logo=next.js&logoColor=white) ![React](https://img.shields.io/badge/React-20232A?style=flat-square&logo=react&logoColor=61DAFB) ![TypeScript](https://img.shields.io/badge/TypeScript-3178C6?style=flat-square&logo=typescript&logoColor=white) ![Tailwind](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tailwind-06B6D4?style=flat-square&logo=tailwindcss&logoColor=white) ![Plaid](https://img.shields.io/badge/Plaid-000000?style=flat-square) ![Neon](https://img.shields.io/badge/Neon-00E599?style=flat-square&logo=neon&logoColor=white)

Self-trained personal finance that actually nags you. Connects your accounts through Plaid, tracks where the money goes, and sends a daily "here's the damage" notice. Next.js + Drizzle on Neon — financial connective tissue, minus the spreadsheet guilt.

### [storydump](https://github.com/chrisrogers37/storydump)
![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white) ![TypeScript](https://img.shields.io/badge/TypeScript-3178C6?style=flat-square&logo=typescript&logoColor=white) ![PostgreSQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/PostgreSQL-4169E1?style=flat-square&logo=postgresql&logoColor=white) ![Telegram](https://img.shields.io/badge/Telegram-26A5E4?style=flat-square&logo=telegram&logoColor=white) ![Instagram](https://img.shields.io/badge/Instagram-E4405F?style=flat-square&logo=instagram&logoColor=white)

Posts to your Instagram Story from a Telegram chat. Drop an image in Telegram, it lands on your Story — the laziest content pipeline I could justify building, by design.

### [benzo](https://github.com/chrisrogers37/benzo)
![Swift](https://img.shields.io/badge/Swift-F05138?style=flat-square&logo=swift&logoColor=white) ![macOS](https://img.shields.io/badge/macOS-000000?style=flat-square&logo=apple&logoColor=white)

Your Mac deserves sleep. A tiny menu-bar app that keeps your Mac awake exactly as long as you want, then lets it rest. Native Swift, no Electron in sight.

### [city-cycles](https://github.com/chrisrogers37/city-cycles)
![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white) ![TypeScript](https://img.shields.io/badge/TypeScript-3178C6?style=flat-square&logo=typescript&logoColor=white)

Comparative analytics for public bike networks across New York and London. Pulls the open feeds, crunches ridership and rebalancing patterns, and tries to explain why one city's bikes are always where you need them and the other's never are.

### [shitpost-alpha](https://github.com/chrisrogers37/shitpost-alpha)
![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white) ![TypeScript](https://img.shields.io/badge/TypeScript-3178C6?style=flat-square&logo=typescript&logoColor=white) ![Truth Social](https://img.shields.io/badge/Truth_Social-5448EE?style=flat-square)

Harvests shitposts from Truth Social and tries to turn them into trading alpha. X and Reddit coming soon. Please forgive the branding. 📈
